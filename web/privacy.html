Privacy policy

Updated October 17, 2026

PromptVault AI is operated by Muhammad Waqar ul Mulk. Contact: muhammadwaqarulmulk@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

You can browse public prompts without an account. If you sign in, Supabase stores your email, authentication records, private boards, bookmarks, saved variables, daily claims, streaks and optional Brand DNA. These records provide account features and enforce usage limits. Your boards and brand profile are private.

Prompt Booster sends the idea you submit to Google Gemini. Image-to-prompt sends your selected, resized reference image to Gemini. Do not submit confidential information or images you do not have permission to use. Reference images are processed in memory by our function and are not saved in our storage. Google may handle requests according to its applicable API terms and retention policies.

Remix uploads are stored privately in Supabase and submitted for manual review. Approved images, captions and vote counts become visible to the community. You may delete your own submissions and report or hide other content. We remove image location metadata in the app before upload.

If you opt into notifications, OneSignal processes push subscription identifiers and device/browser information to deliver reminders. Notification consent is optional and may be revoked in device or browser settings. Store subscriptions use RevenueCat and the platform store; web checkout uses Lemon Squeezy when configured. We store entitlement status, not card details.

Other services and local data

Cloudflare hosts public app and catalog files. Images load from Unsplash. These services receive ordinary network information such as IP addresses and request metadata. The app caches public prompts, images and your bookmarks on your device. Signing out clears the current account's cached bookmarks. External AI launch buttons copy your prompt and open the selected provider; that provider's policies apply.

Retention and deletion

Account content is kept until you remove it or delete the account. Usage reservation records are pruned after 30 days when you use quota features. Payment event identifiers may remain to prevent duplicate webhook processing; they do not contain your email or payment card. Provider logs, backups and data already processed by external providers follow those providers' retention schedules.

Delete your PromptVault AI account and app data. Deletion removes your authentication account, private boards, saved prompts, brand profile, claims, streaks, votes and remix files. It is permanent. Cancel any paid subscription separately with the store; deleting your app account does not cancel billing. Previously issued image links can remain usable for up to 15 minutes.

Your choices

You may use public browsing without signing in, decline notifications, delete individual bookmarks or submissions, and request access, correction or deletion by contacting the address above. We do not sell personal information. PromptVault AI is intended for adults and is not designed for children under 13.
